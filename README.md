I cannot bear the thought of watching you die.
My heart was not made for that hour—
it would break before the world ever could.
 
If fate must come for one of us first,
then let it come for me.
Let me go ahead of you, my love,
so I never have to learn the weight
of a morning without you,
never have to hold your hand
as the warmth drains from it,
never have to say goodbye
to the only home I have ever known in another person.
 
I would rather carry death
than carry the grief of losing you.
So if the stars are cruel enough to demand a price,
I will pay it first.
 
Let me die before you—
not because I want to leave you,
but because I cannot survive staying after you.
I want to be the one who goes first,
so that I never have to live a single breath
in a world where you are gone.
 
If one of us must be left behind,
let it be you—
and let me be the one
who never has to miss you.

I would rather walk into the unknown first
than live one breath in a world where you are gone.
So if the stars are cruel enough to demand a price, I will pay it first.

Let me die before you—
not because I want to leave you,
but because I cannot survive staying after you.
My love, I am not asking to be brave. I am asking to be spared.

I want to go first.
I want to be the one who never has to see.
Because if you leave before me,
I will not die with you—I will only keep living, and that is the cruelest thing of all.
